About this map

Western Province Disaster Watch brings official datasets and community reports together for Colombo, Gampaha and Kalutara districts, to help residents and planners see where floods, landslides and storm damage are happening.

How to use it

  • Layers: turn datasets on and off and change the basemap.
  • Report: tap the map, choose what happened, add a photo if you can, and submit anonymously.
  • Insights: counts by district, Cyclone Ditwah flood footprint, flood hotspots and landslide access.
  • Live: weather forecast and modelled river flow.

Privacy

No name, phone number, e-mail or account is collected. Your report contains the location you choose, your answers and an optional photo. Photos are resized on your device and camera location data is removed. Reports are reviewed before publishing and sensitive ones are not shown.

Data

Administrative boundaries: UN OCHA / Survey Department of Sri Lanka (COD). Hospitals and police stations: Sri Lanka NSDI / Survey Department. Water bodies and wetlands: OpenStreetMap contributors. Cyclone Ditwah flood footprint, landslide locations and soil conservation areas: course dataset provided for this assignment. Weather and river discharge: Open-Meteo.com (river data from GloFAS). Basemaps: CARTO, OpenStreetMap, Esri.
